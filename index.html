Under the absolute gauge of industrial rationality, listen to the silent gasps within the concrete crevices.
Oxygen from photosynthesis is trapped under a thick layer, making the tiny algae puff up a little as they breathe.
In this small world, life is trying to break through an invisible wall. Every quiet push and breath mirrors the countless wanderers caught in the cracks of the city.
As the city bulldozes outward, people are uprooted. Like drifting spores, they are swept into the unknown.
Roaring assembly lines by day, cramped quarters by night. Inside this vast machine, a person is reduced to a data point, a cog.
But just like that trapped oxygen—even under the crushing weight of it all, these drifters fight to breathe. They push back, carving out a little room to survive in the cracks in the concrete.
[ URBAN MICROALGAE NETWORK CONNECTING ]
The 3-axis gantry—the bedrock of China’s manufacturing boom. From phone cases to home appliances, it churned out an era.
For the millions flooding the cities, this was the behemoth they knew best. Day and night. The relentless grinding of its tracks became the soundtrack of their factory lives.
Cold. Precise. It mapped out Z-axis borders and rigid CAD grids. And right there, trapped in the mechanical roar, the youth, sweat, and breath of a generation were deeply embedded.
Twelve concrete slabs. Twelve tiny worlds, locked in a mechanical grid.
Trapped in this tight blockade, the algae fall back on life’s raw geometry. They push through the cold gaps, seeking one continuous route out.
As they spread, their paths become the city’s highways. This is the true proof of life: breaking the limits, and carving out a sanctuary in the cold concrete.

The concrete slabs, tracing the paths of life, are ultimately torn apart and stacked high, rising into a ruined concrete jungle.
It is a suffocating tomb, yet a monument to survival.
Within these relics of algae and concrete, every faint green pulse is made of flesh and blood.
It is the restless, unending breath of the drifters, echoing endlessly across this colossal city.

COLONIZATION & GASPING: A Biological Topology

This is an experiment in confined survival, reconciliation, and the reconstruction of self-narrative. The work focuses on the drifters amidst rapid urbanization—like microalgae scattered in the oppressive crevices of concrete. In these suffocating ruins, the microalgae utilize precise biological morphogenesis to seek optimized continuous paths.

As the most foundational equipment in early manufacturing, the 3-axis gantry represents absolute rationality, precision, and ruthless constraint. When used to guide microalgae, a profound duality is born. The green paths that sprawl out are not only the oxygen supply network but also structurally isomorphic to modern urban grids. Under the iron law of machinery, life still calculates its optimal path.
